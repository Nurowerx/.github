# Epistemic Trust Lab — Founder-Led Independent Research

**Researcher:** [Spencer H. Walker](https://github.com/SWALKERNURO) · November 2026  
**Field:** Human–AI interaction, information science, trustworthy AI, epistemology.

**[Read the canonical project overview](https://github.com/SWALKERNURO/SWALKERNURO/blob/main/projects/EPISTEMIC-TRUST-LAB.md)**

Epistemic Trust Lab is an independent, locally running research instrument developed by Nurowerx founder Spencer H. Walker to examine the gap between **apparent AI understanding** and **observed reliability**. This document records the founder's research activity; it is not a claim that Nurowerx, CU Boulder, or the University of Denver formally sponsored an approved study.

## Reported local build

- **240** synthetic scenarios.
- **5,760** deterministic policy evaluations.
- Interactive dashboard with memory/evidence inspection, metrics, uncertainty intervals, and export support.
- **34 passing automated tests**: 29 backend, 2 frontend, 3 browser, including accessibility checks.
- Local clean-install reproduction of results and summary statistics reported.

The outcomes apply to **deterministic policies on synthetic fixtures**. They do **not** demonstrate performance on live language models, changes in actual users' trust, or a completed human-subject study. The full code archive and research report are not currently published online.

The research aligns intellectually with Nurowerx's interest in explainable AI, memory provenance, information integrity, human agency, and the visibility of uncertainty, but should be attributed to the independent researcher unless formal institutional ownership or sponsorship is later documented.
